# Educ-up

> Educ-up is a French social-impact education group founded in 2016 by Mohamed El Mazzouji,
> headquartered in Saint-Denis, La Réunion with establishments in metropolitan France. It sells
> education *services*, not software: Domissori places Montessori-trained educators in family
> homes for childcare, workshops and tutoring, and Edacademy is a Qualiopi-certified training and
> apprenticeship centre for early-childhood and personal-care professions. **Educ-up publishes no
> API, no developer portal and no machine-readable contract.** This file was generated by API
> Evangelist from the company's public surface; Educ-up does not serve an llms.txt of its own.

## What Educ-up is

- Group founded 2016; founder Mohamed El Mazzouji; French SIREN 822074258.
- Headquarters 45 Rue Alexis de Villeneuve, Saint-Denis (97400), La Réunion; secondary
  establishment 3 Rue de Rosny, Montreuil (93100).
- Two brands: Domissori (in-home Montessori childcare and tutoring, launched 2019) and
  Edacademy (Qualiopi-certified training centre / CFA).
- Ed'solidaire is the group's subsidy scheme opening paid childcare to low-income families.
- Investors include Serena, M Capital, Inco Ventures, MakeSense and Racine2.

## Brands and sites

- [Domissori](https://domissori.fr/): in-home childcare, educational workshops and academic
  tutoring across Vannes, Chambéry, Lyon, Grenoble, Perpignan, Montpellier, Marseille and
  Saint-Denis. Quote-based (https://domissori.fr/devis/); WordPress site.
- [Edacademy](https://www.edacademy.fr/): CAP AEPE, Titre Pro ADVF, Bac Pro ASSP, Montessori and
  parenting-support programmes, apprenticeship placement, employer training. Webflow site.
- [Edacademy learning platform](https://formation.edacademy.fr/): the self-hosted Moodle LMS
  Edacademy built out from 2021 to deliver its training online. Login-gated for enrolled learners.
- [Domissori blog](https://domissori.fr/blog/) and [Edacademy blog](https://www.edacademy.fr/blog).

## Developer surface

- **None.** There is no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/MCP
  surface, no SDK in any package registry, no status page and no changelog.
- The corporate domain https://educ-up.fr/ no longer serves a site: it 302s to
  http://s880385942.onlinehome.fr/, an IONOS shared-hosting default page that answers HTTP 200
  with the same 336-byte body for every path, including nonsense paths. Treat any 200 from that
  host as a soft-404, not a document.
- Two machine-readable endpoints exist on live hosts, and both are stock platform defaults rather
  than anything Educ-up published: the WordPress core REST index at https://domissori.fr/wp-json/
  on the Domissori marketing site, and the Moodle web-service endpoint at
  https://formation.edacademy.fr/webservice/rest/server.php, which answers HTTP 200 with
  `{"exception":"moodle_exception","errorcode":"invalidtoken"}` — enabled, but token-gated and
  undocumented. Neither is a product API, neither is supported for third-party use, and no spec for
  either has been saved into this profile.
- A separately-owned US edtech company trades as **EducUp** on educup.com / educup.io and
  github.com/educup. It is a different company. Do not attribute its apps, repositories or
  packages to this Educ-up.
